Installation of VMware on your Laptop.

VMware is free to use for the personal usage.

[Blog Post](https://blogs.vmware.com/teamfusion/2024/05/fusion-pro-now-available-free-for-personal-use.html)

Fusion Pro is designed for macOS, while Workstation Pro is meant for Windows and Linux users.

First, You need to create an account on the https://support.broadcom.com

Register using your email e.g. username@gmail.com.

After successful account registration download the VMware by accepting terms and conditions check box.

If your base operating system for your machine is MacOS then use Fusion Pro.
[Download](https://support.broadcom.com/group/ecx/productdownloads?subfamily=VMware+Fusion)

If your base operating system for your machine is Windows or Linux PC then use Workstation Pro.
[Download](https://support.broadcom.com/group/ecx/productdownloads?subfamily=VMware+Workstation+Pro)
